WaveSync for Adobe Premiere Pro
===============================

WaveSync lines up your camera clips and separately recorded audio by
matching their waveforms, like PluralEyes. It works right inside the
Premiere Pro timeline. It handles multicam shoots, podcasts, and
interviews, including shoots with many separate takes.

Works with Premiere Pro 2022 and newer.


INSTALL
-------

1. Install ffmpeg (free; WaveSync uses it to read audio). One time only.

   Mac:      Install Homebrew from https://brew.sh, then open Terminal
             and run:   brew install ffmpeg

   Windows:  Open Command Prompt and run:   winget install Gyan.FFmpeg

2. Unzip this folder, then double-click the installer:

   Mac:      "Install WaveSync (Mac).command"
             If macOS says it can't be opened: right-click the file,
             choose Open, then click Open again.

   Windows:  "Install WaveSync (Windows).bat"
             If Windows SmartScreen appears: click "More info",
             then "Run anyway".

3. Restart Premiere Pro and open:  Window > Extensions > WaveSync


HOW TO USE
----------

1. Put your clips in a sequence (rough placement is fine):
     - each camera on its own track (Cam A on V1, Cam B on V2...)
     - each audio recorder on its own audio track
   Takes from the same camera or recorder go on the same track.

2. Select the clips, or choose "All clips in the sequence".

3. Keep "Back up the sequence first" ticked, and press Sync.

WaveSync groups matching camera clips and audio takes into scenes,
syncs each scene, and lays the scenes out in order on the timeline.

Results:
  green   = synced
  yellow  = synced, but give it a quick check
  red     = no match found (the clip is placed in order, but not synced)

To undo: open the backup sequence ("<your sequence> - Backup")
in your Project panel. Your original sequence stays open.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

- Clips with speed changes, nested sequences, and graphics are skipped.
- WaveSync won't move a clip on top of another clip. If something is
  in the way, it stops and tells you which clips.
- Very long takes from devices with drifting clocks show a "drift"
  note. Drift is reported but not corrected yet.
- To uninstall, delete the "com.wavesync.panel" folder from:
    Mac:      ~/Library/Application Support/Adobe/CEP/extensions/
    Windows:  %APPDATA%\Adobe\CEP\extensions\
